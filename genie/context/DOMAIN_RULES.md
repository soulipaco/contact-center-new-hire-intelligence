# Domain Rules

## Rule 1: Preserve weighted KPI math

Aggregate scores as the sum of raw numerators divided by the sum of denominators.

## Rule 2: Respect KPI direction

AHT, ACW, and Hold Time are lower-is-better. QA Score, QA Pass Rate, CSAT, FCR,
and Adherence are higher-is-better.

## Rule 3: Gate weak learning curves

R-squared below 0.30 is inconclusive. Do not present its target day as a reliable
operating commitment.

## Rule 4: Carry reliability context

Pair performance conclusions with volume quartile, denominator, and distinct-agent
coverage.

## Rule 5: Treat forecasts as scenarios

Show the point estimate and 80 percent interval. Forecasts support planning and do
not establish causation.
