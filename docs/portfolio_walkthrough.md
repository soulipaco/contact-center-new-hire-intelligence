# Portfolio presentation walkthrough

## Ninety-second version

“This project asks how quickly new contact-center cohorts become production-ready
and how confident we should be in that conclusion. I built a privacy-safe Databricks
lakehouse with temporal dimensions, direction-aware targets, volume reliability,
and tenured sigma benchmarks. For every fictional cohort and KPI, four learning-
curve families compete in MLflow; the 120 winners are packaged into one governed
Unity Catalog portfolio model. Prophet adds a six-month planning interval. Five
semantic views feed a Git-managed Genie space, while governed presentation views feed
a nine-page AI/BI dashboard. The deployment-kit pattern then adds a reviewed
operating playbook, Vector Search retrieval, Genie Deep Research, and persisted LLM
action plans. Jobs, dashboard, Genie, metadata, evaluation, and docs all deploy from
one Asset Bundle; the workspace UI is the demo surface, not the source of truth.”

## Suggested live demo

1. Start with the architecture and explain why weighted scores matter.
2. Open the bootstrap workflow and show the parallel ML/forecast branches.
3. Trace the Gold fact back through Silver temporal joins to Bronze synthetic inputs.
4. Show one cohort’s four candidate runs, then the UC portfolio-model manifest.
5. On Executive Summary, filter one fictional program and KPI.
6. Open Insight & Action Center to show the persisted, retrieval-grounded recommendation.
7. Use Learning & Volume to compare observed learning, selected fit, target timing,
   weekly production exposure, and cumulative handled volume.
8. Use Drivers & Regression to discuss KPI-tenure and KPI-volume association without
   claiming causality.
9. Use Process Control and Outliers to separate target misses, special causes, and
   unusual agent-weeks using direction-aware z-scores and IQR fences.
10. Use Forecast to discuss the point estimate and both 80 percent boundaries.
11. Ask Genie a lower-is-better AHT question and inspect the generated SQL.
12. Open the generated operating playbook and show how retrieval turns diagnosis
    into immediate actions, short-term actions, and measurement targets.
13. Finish with the fail-fast acceptance task, 12/12 Genie result, and privacy gate.

## Interview discussion points

- Why raw numerators and denominators are preserved instead of averaging percentages.
- Why SCD2 and effective-dated targets prevent historical restatement.
- Why R-squared supports model selection but does not prove causality.
- Why a single UC portfolio model is more governable than 120 registered objects.
- Why low-volume quartiles and distinct-agent counts belong beside performance.
- Why KPI-volume and KPI-tenure regressions are associations rather than causal claims.
- Why z-score and IQR methods complement one another for outlier detection.
- Why the operational FPY/DPMO definition is useful without overstating formal Six Sigma capability.
- Why forecast uncertainty drives scenarios rather than a single staffing promise.
- Why reviewed retrieval content is safer than unconstrained “AI recommendations.”
- Why confidential requirements can inform design without entering the public data path.
- Why Git is the source of truth and Databricks is the execution environment.
